# Shift Watch Later for YouTube

Quickly add or remove YouTube videos from your Watch Later playlist by Shift + Left Clicking thumbnails.

## Features

- **Instant Watch Later Toggle:** Hold `Shift` and left-click any YouTube video or Shorts thumbnail to add or remove it from Watch Later without opening menus.
- **Hover Overlay Preview:** Holding `Shift` over a thumbnail highlights it with a clear add/saved indicator so you can see its current status before clicking.
- **Saved Corner Badge:** Videos already in your Watch Later playlist display a subtle corner bookmark marker on their thumbnails. Click the corner marker directly to remove the video from Watch Later.
- **Invert Controls Mode:** Open the extension popup and enable **Invert Controls** to swap the behavior (normal left-click toggles Watch Later; `Shift + Left Click` opens the video).
- **Native Browser Controls Preserved:** Middle-click, right-click, and `Ctrl` / `Alt` / `Meta` + Click always keep their standard browser behavior.
- **Real-Time Sync & In-Page Feedback:** Displays lightweight in-page toast notifications and stays synchronized when you add or remove videos through YouTube's built-in UI.

## Requirements

- You must be signed in to your YouTube account in Chrome to add or remove videos from Watch Later.

## Privacy

- No external servers, analytics, telemetry, or third-party tracking.
- All Watch Later requests go directly from your browser to `https://www.youtube.com` using your existing YouTube session.
- Extension settings and a local Watch Later badge cache are stored strictly in Chrome's built-in extension storage (`chrome.storage.sync` and `chrome.storage.local`).
