Privacy Policy

Shift Watch Later for YouTube

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 Applies to: Chrome Extension (Manifest V3)

Shift Watch Later for YouTube is an independent, unofficial browser extension. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube or Google LLC.

1. Summary (No Data Collection)

Shift Watch Later for YouTube does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or usage data with the developer or any third party.

The extension has no external backend server, no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking scripts. All functionality runs strictly inside your browser while you use https://www.youtube.com/.

2. Data Processed Locally in Your Browser

To add or remove videos from your YouTube Watch Later playlist and display saved-state badges on thumbnails, the extension accesses and processes the following information solely within your browser:

  • YouTube Session & Client Configuration: When you are signed in to YouTube, the extension reads the SAPISID (or __Secure-3PAPISID) cookie from document.cookie and reads YouTube's in-page client configuration (window.ytcfg). These values are used in memory only to compute the SAPISIDHASH authorization header required for same-origin requests to https://www.youtube.com/youtubei/v1/browse and https://www.youtube.com/youtubei/v1/browse/edit_playlist, and to derive a truncated 16-character SHA-1 hash for detecting YouTube account switches locally. Raw cookie values and credentials are never stored or transmitted outside your direct same-origin requests to YouTube.
  • Watch Later Playlist Identifiers: The extension reads video IDs (videoId) and playlist entry IDs (setVideoId) from your Watch Later (WL) playlist, and passively observes YouTube's own same-origin edit_playlist requests for playlistId: "WL" so thumbnail badges stay synchronized when you edit Watch Later through YouTube's native UI.
  • Extension Preferences: Your chosen click behavior mode (watch_later or open_video) configured in the extension popup.

3. Local Browser Storage

The extension uses Chrome's built-in storage APIs strictly to persist your preference and to cache Watch Later badges locally across page loads and tabs:

Storage Area Key Stored Data & Purpose
chrome.storage.sync shiftClickAction Stores your Invert Controls preference (watch_later or open_video) so it syncs across Chrome profiles where Chrome Sync is enabled.
chrome.storage.local swl_cache Stores a local cache of your Watch Later video ID pairs (videoId / setVideoId), the last sync timestamp (ts), and a 16-character local SHA-1 hash (account) used to clear stale cache entries if you switch YouTube accounts. This data never leaves your device.

4. Permissions & Host Access

Shift Watch Later for YouTube requests only the minimal permissions required to deliver its single purpose:

Permission / Scope Purpose
storage Reads and writes your click-mode preference in chrome.storage.sync and your local Watch Later badge cache in chrome.storage.local, and listens for chrome.storage.onChanged events to keep open YouTube tabs in sync.
https://www.youtube.com/* (Content Scripts) Injects inject.js (MAIN world) to bridge window.ytcfg and observe native Watch Later edits, and injects content.js and styles.css (ISOLATED world) to handle Shift + Click actions, render thumbnail badges, hover overlays, and in-page toast notifications, and call YouTube's same-origin playlist endpoints. The extension does not run on or access any other website.

5. Third-Party Services & External Links

The extension does not communicate with any third-party servers, APIs, or analytics providers. Network requests initiated by the extension go exclusively to https://www.youtube.com using your existing browser session.

The extension popup includes an optional Sponsor link pointing to https://github.com/sponsors/uniunigoduni. No data is sent to GitHub unless you explicitly click that link, at which point GitHub's own Privacy Statement applies.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because no data is collected on any remote server, you have full control over all data stored by the extension:

  • Uninstalling the extension from chrome://extensions removes all locally stored cache data (chrome.storage.local) and synced extension settings (chrome.storage.sync).
  • Clearing extension storage via Chrome Developer Tools or signing out / switching YouTube accounts immediately invalidates the local Watch Later cache.

7. Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

The use of information accessed by Shift Watch Later for YouTube adheres strictly to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information accessed on YouTube is used solely to provide the user-facing Watch Later toggle and badge functionality and is never transferred, sold, or used for advertising, profiling, or creditworthiness.
